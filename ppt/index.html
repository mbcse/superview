SuperView is a social desk for market ideas. You type a view. An agent finds eligible companies, builds a basket, and shows whether that idea is winning on live markets.

The problem

People have opinions about markets. Almost nothing turns those opinions into a testable portfolio.

Today an idea dies in ChatGPT tabs, Twitter threads, and brokerage watchlists. Nobody can cleanly answer: was that view actually good?

Ideas stay as text

A hot take never becomes a sized basket you can hold.

Research is messy

Everyone picks names differently. Risk limits are optional or missing.

No scoreboard

FinTwit is loud. Almost nobody posts a view with a live S&P comparison.

Social ≠ portfolio

You can like a take. You cannot fund it, copy it, or prove it later.

The solution

SuperView turns one sentence into a live, benchmarked portfolio.

Why this is different — and the features that make it stick.

Not another AI chat that dumps ticker names. Not another brokerage skin. A full path from belief to book.

Core object The View One sentence, researched holdings, live performance, comments, and invest — all on the same object. That is the product.
Feature Research agent

Multi-step pipeline: interpret → screen → diligence → size → critic. If too few names fit, it refuses instead of faking a book.

Feature Hard constraints

Cash, company, sector, and role caps live in code. The model suggests. Rules decide what actually gets into the basket.

Feature Live vs S&P 500

Every published view carries a scoreboard from day one. Opinions get a mark, not vibes.

Feature Paper ledger

Simulated USDG with live Robinhood prices. Practice the idea before real money is on the line.

Feature Locked receipts

Publishing hashes the basket. The original book cannot quietly change after the fact.

Feature Investing feed

Follow ideas, not only people. Copy a view, comment on it, or put paper money behind it.
