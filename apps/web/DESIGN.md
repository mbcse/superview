# SuperView visual system

Lagoon on mist. Saans for SuperView, headlines, and view sentences. Geist for UI, figures, and meta. The view is the hero.

## Thesis

App opens on the feed. A short view becomes a researched basket. An agent manages it.

## Type

Geist for UI. Saans for SuperView, headlines, and view sentences. Tabular Geist (`.figure`) for prices and vs S&P. Geist Mono only for tiny meta. No italics.

Page title 28/32. Section 20. Post body 16. Body 15. Meta 13. Label 11.

## Motion

Glide `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`. Card lift 220ms. Research enter 250ms. Comments bottom sheet 320ms from below. Landing hero loops a Remotion view→basket→invest sequence (15s). How it works, stock tokens, and vs S&P sections loop their own Remotion films. Price flash 150ms. Reduced motion: still of the last beat. Offscreen players pause.
