# SuperView visual system

Lagoon on mist. Geist sans everywhere — titles, view sentences, and figures. The view is the hero.

## Thesis

App opens on the feed. A short view becomes a researched basket. An agent manages it.

White translucent glass (`glass-chrome`, `glass-card`, `glass-sheet`) with teal hairline borders. Research overlay is abyss + planet. Primary CTA is the lagoon gradient (teal → sky-deep).

## Type

Geist sans only. Weight 600 and tight tracking for titles. Tabular sans (`.figure`) for prices and vs S&P. Geist Mono only for tiny meta, not hero numbers. No italics.

Page title 28/32. Section 20. Post body 16. Body 15. Meta 13. Label 11.

## Motion

Glide `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`. Card lift 220ms. Research enter 250ms. Price flash 150ms. Reduced motion: instant.
